Ready for Supervisor / Senior Chef

Assessing readiness for the next step through our values, behaviours and practical experience.

How to use this assessment

Score each behaviour from 1–5 using examples you have genuinely observed during normal shifts. Consider how consistently the behaviour is demonstrated, especially during busy or challenging situations. Use the evidence box to describe a specific shift, task, conversation or situation that supports the score.

  • Every behaviour must be scored.
  • The overall average must be above 4.0.
  • No individual behaviour can be scored below 3.
  • All practical tasks must be completed.
